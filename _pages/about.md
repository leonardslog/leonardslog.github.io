Hello, I am a researcher who uses computational and statistical methods to answer big questions and visualize complex answers. My academic training is in evolutionary biology (preferred system: reptiles and amphibians but especially snakes), but I'm an equal opportunity data wrangler. Here you'll find a mix of my  research interests and forays into analyses, summaries, and visualizations of topics that interest me. Above all else, this site is a sandbox for me to explore new ideas and share my exploration of new methods and questions. Feel free to drop me a line if you have any questions or comments.
